# 🎓 D.El.Ed Buddy

> Your friendly companion for D.El.Ed.

**D.El.Ed Buddy** is a free, student-created learning platform for Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed.) students.

It brings together study notes, detailed notes, quick revision material, infographics, previous-year questions, quizzes, practical resources, and other useful study materials in one place.

The project is being built gradually alongside the actual D.El.Ed. course, starting with **Semester 1**.

---

## ✨ Why D.El.Ed Buddy?

D.El.Ed students often have to search through PDFs, WhatsApp groups, websites, handwritten notes, and different study materials just to prepare for one unit.

D.El.Ed Buddy aims to make that process simpler.

The goal is to create a **student-friendly, organized, and completely free study companion** where students can quickly find the material they need without digging through dozens of files.

This isn't intended to replace textbooks, classroom teaching, or official curriculum documents.

It is a companion to them.
