The SIP marks are part of the semester total. They are not added on top of 500.

## Where the SIP marks sit

The programme table allocates 10 SIP marks each to **S1.1, S1.4, S1.7, S1.8 and S1.9**. Together, those make 50 marks.

This graphic reproduces the supplied 2024 table. Use your institution’s current instructions for assessment decisions.
