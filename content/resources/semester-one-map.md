## The numbers to keep handy

| Semester 1 | In the curriculum |
| --- | --- |
| Courses | S1.1 to S1.9 |
| Credits | 20 |
| Total marks | 500 |
| External assessment | 270 marks |
| Internal assessment, excluding SIP column | 180 marks |
| SIP allocation | 50 marks, included in the 500 total |
| Expected study hours | 500 |
| School Internship - I | Two weeks |

S1.1 carries 4 credits and 100 marks. Each of S1.2–S1.9 carries 2 credits and 50 marks.

## Read the marks correctly

The programme table has separate external, internal and SIP columns. The detailed course pages sometimes combine internal and SIP marks. For example, S1.1 shows **70 + 20 + 10 = 100** in the table and **70 + 30 = 100** in the detailed paper.

The 50 SIP marks are already part of the semester’s 500 marks. Do not add them again.
