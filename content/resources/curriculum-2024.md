## Your source of truth

This is the supplied **Revised Curriculum-Syllabus 2024** published by SCERT Assam. It covers the full two-year programme. This website currently organizes only Semester 1 as active study content.

Use the document’s own wording for course requirements. The website’s course summaries are navigation aids; they do not replace the curriculum or your institution’s instructions.

## Differences worth checking with your teacher

- **S1.1 has two titles.** The programme table and detailed paper use different headings. The website follows the programme table and preserves a note about the detailed heading.
- **The internship table labels its first activity “ECCE (S1.1)”.** The course list uses S1.2 for ECCE. The detailed S1.1 SIP activity also differs from this table. Confirm the required activity with your mentor.
- **S1.9 says “Any two” under SIP activities but lists one numbered item.** The site does not invent the missing task.
